# Reflection 

Creating this portfolio has been quite a journey, and honestly, I never expected to make it this far. Looking back from the first mission up to the latest one, I can clearly see how much my skills and understanding have improved along the way. Now that I have the opportunity to reflect on everything, I would like to share my experiences and thoughts about each mission.

## Mission 1
I would say this was the stage where I was still figuring things out. Although I was able to follow the lesson and complete the required activities, I overlooked some important tasks afterward, such as adding my personal reflection and creating a proper About Me section. It was a good starting point, but there were still things I needed to improve.

## Mission 2
During this mission, I started gaining a better understanding of the topics, particularly technical documentation. I became more interested in learning how to properly organize and present technical information. However, I also realized that professionalism was still one of my weaknesses, especially when it came to presenting my work.

## Mission 3
This mission focused mainly on exploring different cloud platforms and understanding the services they offer. It also involved analyzing which platforms and services would be most suitable for specific needs. Overall, I found this mission relatively straightforward, although much of the work required careful research and analysis.

## Mission 4
This was where I started becoming more engaged with the lessons. Docker containers and related technologies were particularly interesting to me because I have always had an interest in them. I began to understand how useful containerization can be when developing, deploying, and managing network services.

## Mission 5
This mission focused more on storage and the underlying components of the host computer. It also helped me understand the advantages of containerization and why managing applications through containers can be much more convenient and efficient.

## Mission 6
I particularly enjoyed this mission because it involved working with configuration files and understanding how different settings affect the overall system. It was a good opportunity to become more familiar with the technical side of configuring services.

## Mission 7
One thing I noticed during this mission was that I had overlooked several spelling mistakes in my previous work. Aside from that, the activities mainly focused on troubleshooting, examining logs, and understanding how to identify and resolve system issues.

## Mission 8
This was another interesting mission, particularly because it focused on firewalls and network security. It helped me better understand how firewall configurations control network traffic and protect systems from unauthorized access.

## Mission 9
This mission introduced me to cron jobs and task scheduling. I learned how scheduled tasks can help automate routine operations and reduce the need for constant manual intervention.

## Mission 10
Finally, we reached the part where we actually built and configured a server from the ground up. This was probably one of my favorite missions because I genuinely enjoyed the process of setting up the infrastructure, configuring services, and making everything work together. It was a very rewarding experience and allowed me to apply many of the skills I had developed throughout the previous missions.

Overall, this entire experience has helped me become more confident and knowledgeable about cloud computing and infrastructure management. I know there are still many things I need to learn and improve, but I am proud of how far I have come. Each mission contributed something valuable to my learning experience, and I look forward to applying these skills to future projects.
