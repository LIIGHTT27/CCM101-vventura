## 💭 Reflection

After completing this mission, the cloud platform that impressed me the most was AWS because of its wide range of services and global infrastructure. I learned that AWS can support many different types of workloads, from simple applications to large enterprise systems. However, I also learned that AWS is not automatically the best choice for every organization. Azure and GCP have their own strengths that can make them better choices depending on the situation.

AWS, Azure, and GCP have many similarities because they all provide cloud computing services such as virtual machines, storage, databases, networking, identity management, and security. They also allow organizations to access resources through management consoles and scale their infrastructure based on demand. The main difference is the way each provider approaches certain technologies and the types of organizations they are especially suited for.

Business requirements have a major influence on cloud platform selection. Factors such as budget, existing technology, performance requirements, scalability, security, and the type of application must be considered. For example, Azure is a good choice for an organization already using Microsoft technologies, while GCP can be a strong choice for AI, machine learning, and Kubernetes workloads.

Acting as a Cloud Solutions Consultant helped me understand that choosing a cloud platform requires more than simply choosing the most popular provider. I had to consider the actual needs of each client and match those needs with the services offered by each cloud platform.

This mission also improved my GitHub Cloud Computing Portfolio because I added research, comparisons, service information, and real-world recommendations. It gave me a better understanding of how cloud platforms are used in actual business situations and helped me organize my work into a more complete portfolio.
