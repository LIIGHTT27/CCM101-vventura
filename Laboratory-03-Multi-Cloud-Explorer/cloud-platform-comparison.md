---

## 🧠 Analyst Q&A

### 1️⃣ Which cloud provider offers the broadest range of services?

> **AWS** offers the broadest overall range of cloud services. It has a large ecosystem covering computing, storage, databases, networking, security, analytics, AI, and many other areas.

### 2️⃣ Which provider best integrates with Microsoft technologies?

> **Microsoft Azure** is the best choice for organizations that already use Microsoft technologies. It integrates well with Windows Server, Microsoft 365, Active Directory/Entra ID, and other Microsoft enterprise products.

### 3️⃣ Which provider is strongest in Artificial Intelligence and Kubernetes?

> **Google Cloud Platform (GCP)** is a strong choice for AI, machine learning, and Kubernetes. Google has extensive experience with AI and developed Kubernetes, making GCP attractive for cloud-native and AI workloads.

### 4️⃣ Which cloud platform would you personally choose, and why?

> **I would personally choose AWS** because it provides a wide range of services and gives me more opportunities to learn different areas of cloud computing. Its large ecosystem would also be useful for developing skills that can be applied to different types of projects.
